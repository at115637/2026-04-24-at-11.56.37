In an infamous YouTube video from 2004, the unidentified motorbike rider known as "Ghost Rider" tore around Paris on the périphérique in just nine minutes, averaging 211 km/h.

Since then, the speed limit has been lowered to 50 km/h. Noise has decreased by roughly 2 dB(A), while NO₂ levels have dropped by around 6%.

Today, a full lap takes roughly 45 minutes.
