you were standing right next to me, dreaming. coming back into the nowness of being when i look over to you, shaking off this constant state of fragmentation. hasty eye movement and then, stagnation again. there, now, before, and the anticipation of coming attractions, coupled with the sounds of longing and faint clarity. I see you looking at the sky, discontinuing the memory visit, dissolving the immediate, actively imagining. "hey, that next song is so good; can you hear how…?"
